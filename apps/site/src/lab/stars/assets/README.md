`stars.json`: the 2,319 stars of magnitude ≤ 5.3 from the Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th rev. (Hoffleit & Warren 1991),
as `[RA°, Dec°, V magnitude, temperature K / 100]` (J2000), brightest first.

Source: https://github.com/brettonw/YaleBrightStarCatalog (`bsc5-short.json`, MIT licence; the catalogue data itself is
public domain, distributed by NASA/ADC and CDS as catalogue V/50). Trimmed and reformatted, no values altered beyond rounding.
